# FORCE-SLICE-QA-001 — real measured currents, arithmetic QA only

**Evidence class:** observed 3-D current-vector data from FORCE/CIOOS Atlantic **retrieved**, but **not scientifically quality-approved**. No turbine model or site-resource forecast has ingested these values.

**Exact acquisition:** GitHub Actions [run 39470715965](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/TidalTurbine/actions/runs/39470715965) checked out temporary probe commit `3a3ffa54998c2a24428c87cf0f22ab7c1392b659`, ran bounded `scripts/qa_force_slice.py --fetch`, and uploaded the source CSV and original numerical QA manifest as artifact `12286987076`, retention **1 day**. The original CSV was independently downloaded from the workflow artifact and its 30,276 bytes matched SHA256 **`1a19da1e3cd214fc904d8e821cc50c7e959693aaffa4dc9c8e84ee6c663ac4cb`**. Publisher [dataset metadata](https://cioosatlantic.ca/erddap/info/FORCE_Mar2018_ADCP_Currents/index.html) indicates CC BY 4.0; credit FORCE/CIOOS Atlantic. The original third-party measurements are deliberately not committed to Git.

**Sample:** March 30, 2018, **20:02:33–22:02:30 UTC**; position **45.3631 N, -64.4275 E**; 9 unique observation times approximately 15 minutes apart; 50 instrument `cell_range` values 1.5–50.5 **metres relative to ADCP transducer**; 450 entries (9×50) with eastward, northward and upward current components in m/s.

**Critical distinction uncovered by independently replaying raw CSV:** `valid_numeric_rows=450` reflects *only successful basic numeric parsing*. **206/450 vectors (45.7778%) are exactly (0,0,0)**. **21/50 range cells (30.5–50.5 m) are all-zero for all nine times.** Some 27.5–29.5 m cells contain partial zero blocks. This is **suspect missing/sidelobe/out-of-water masking**, not verified evidence of stagnant water. We cannot identify the mechanism without manufacturer files, sensor tilt/heading, pressure/water level, beam correlation, signal-to-noise, and source-correct QC flags. Zero vectors are explicitly *unresolved*, not automatically discarded or classified as true missing data.

**QA arithmetic:** 450 parseable, 0 parsing/out-of-range/duplicate rejections under generic bounds, **244 nonzero and 206 exact-zero**. File checksum, units, date range, geolocation and representative counts are in `reports/FORCE-SLICE-QA-001.json`. Generic absolute-speed ceilings of 15 m/s are **screening only**, not calibrated ADCP uncertainty or physical validation. No device depth, safe swept zone, annual statistics, power-density prediction, extreme-current recurrence or velocity-profile curve is validated.

**Reproduce without Git-hosted raw data:** Download [artifact](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/TidalTurbine/actions/runs/39470715965/artifacts/12286987076) while retained, then run `python scripts/review_force_artifact.py --zip path/to/downloaded.zip`. Or retain `source.csv` outside Git and run `python scripts/review_force_artifact.py --csv data/local/force_adcp_slice/source.csv`. Program enforces the original SHA256; if re-fetched publisher data differ, halt for manual provenance reconciliation. Offline synthetic fixture tests only test parser behavior; they do not scientifically validate ADCP vectors.

**Gate before numerical turbine modelling:** obtain instrument deployment geometry, sea surface/pressure and tilt, valid-bin masks/quality flags and publisher QA. Acquire multiple complete semidiurnal cycles and longer seasonal representative observations with uncertainty; keep raw data separately and reviewed derivative hashes in Git. Do **not** assume the 244 nonzero values are usable without these checks. Avoid interpreting 1.5–50.5 m as height above bottom without instrument geometry.
