# Unranked prototype comparison — independent acceptance gates

Canonical machine-readable experiment plan: [`research/prototype_gates.json`](../research/prototype_gates.json); validator: `python scripts/check_prototypes.py`.

Three different **energy-conversion mechanisms** will undergo common **measurement** conditions while retaining appropriate separate capture-area definitions. These are *not three equivalent calibrated turbine models*. All three lack qualified FORCE site inflow and common-site net electrical energy. No weighted scoring, winner or claim of physical validation.

| Dimension | Axial RM1-type | Cross-flow RM2 H-rotor | Oscillating nonrotating foil |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary capture | lifting axial rotor | lifting transverse rotor | lift-driven heave/pitch, reciprocating |
| Benchmark evidence | 2014 RM1 1:40 dual axial lab mechanical Cp vs TSR | 2016 UNH RM2 1:6 cross-flow processed mechanical Cp, Cd, run variability | bookkeeping model only; no independently curated net power history |
| Area / energy denominator | rotor disk (count individual rotors) | frontal projected D×H; not disk | actual foil travel span × stroke plus shared control volume |
| PTO and controls | fixed laboratory rotor, controller; real electrical system unspecified | controlled TSR in towing tank, not full power conversion | heave/pitch actuator consumption and electromechanical or hydraulic PTO explicitly metered |
| Structural acceptance | instantaneous blade-root stress and rotor thrust required | cyclic blade/strut forces and phase torque required | time-resolved heave force and pitch moment and material fatigue required |
| Access/maintenance | submerged pitch, gearbox/seals/support | cross-flow bearings/struts or float retrieval depending design | flexing links, hinges/cylinders, seals and storm-parkability |
| Environmental unresolved | collision, debris, noise, habitat/wake | swept-area collision and support entanglement, turbulence | foil sweep, trapping/pinch, control motion, habitat |

**Prospective lab envelope is an engineering assumption, not field data:** nominal inflow setpoints 0.6, 1.0, 1.4 m/s, both directions, with measured actual inflow, turbulence and calibration. Repeat all experimental conditions enough to establish confidence, randomization and failure thresholds prospectively. Match test-section blockage and upstream measurement domain. Compare **net delivered electricity per actual occupied corridor and period** only after normalizing stream energy boundaries, auxiliary consumption, downtime and tested hydrodynamic conditions; raw Cp from different area denominators is not a ranking.

### Individual stop/proceed gates, never a single opaque score

- **Hydrodynamics:** reproduce *published data* separately from independent solver validation. Assess reversal, slack, cavitation and high turbulence, with validated scale law and uncertainty.
- **Environment:** DFO monitoring, Mi'kmaq participation, ecological observation/detection limits, marine navigation, fish collision and trapping, noise, habitat and cumulative effects. Missing permission blocks field work independently of performance.
- **Access/service:** compare recovery windows, demonstrated repair procedures, vessel and operator risks, connection/mooring/hinge failure evidence, downtime and replacement parts. Unknown = pending, not zero cost.
- **Manufacture/end of life:** actual tooling/materials, reproducibility, seals/bearings and fatigue, coating and biofouling, material provenance and independently supported decommissioning pathway.
- **Energy systems:** grid dispatch and flexible AI computing loads remain a separate study; tidal slack persists. No claim of continuous power.

**Decision today:** do not select a winner. Advance RM1 hydraulic reference table and RM2 load measurements, complete qualified FORCE water-column QC, and run an independent net-energy oscillating-foil test before committing design capital or hardware. This plan authorizes no external testing, contacting, purchasing or deployment.
