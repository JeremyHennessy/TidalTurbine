## Freeze before the test, not after looking at its result

This is a prospective protocol specification. The actual solver, acceptance numbers, original held-out run IDs, hashes, executable environment and replicate design are **not yet selected**. No run is currently eligible to be called a blinded confirmation.

1. **Define one physical observable and application domain.** For a first laboratory prediction, choose mechanical Cp/torque or support drag with exact rotor, shaft position, sign, SI units, denominator and averaging operation. Preserve RM1 left/right rotor identity, local inflow asymmetry and its own disk area; RM2 projected D×H is a different denominator. Do not silently mix mechanical, electrical, thrust, stress and fatigue observables. State geometry, Reynolds number, blockage, free surface, inflow statistics and actuator boundary. A validated Cp model alone cannot validate force or stress.
2. **Freeze development/calibration versus test groups.** Pin original dataset bytes and source licence, instrument version, complete trial/deployment IDs and a machine-readable split manifest. Entire runs and shared calibration/deployment groups remain together: never randomly split adjacent time samples, revolutions or repeated processing of the same trial into train/test. Audit parameter fitting, geometry choices, preprocessing, hyperparameter selection and prior manual inspection for leakage. Existing inspected tables/trials are disclosed as exposed. A test group previously consulted cannot become independent merely by renaming it. Use genuinely unconsulted measurements and independent assessment where possible; record limitations if the evidence is retrospective rather than blinded.
3. **Freeze the computational model.** Record exact code commit, solver equations and numerical settings, dependencies/environment, random seeds, meshes/time steps and convergence criteria, parameter values/units/source and calibration objective. Keep verification (code/math/numerical convergence) distinct from agreement with experiment. Calibrating parameters to the eventual validation targets invalidates the holdout. A later change requires a new version and fresh eligible holdout, while preserving the earlier result.
4. **Freeze data processing and exclusions.** Pin coordinate transforms, time alignment, sensor calibration/offsets, anti-alias filters/resampling, startup/steady-state definitions, gaps/saturation/exclusion reasons and QC thresholds. Set tolerances from instrument/physical evidence before observing effects. Never infer source flags from nonzero speed, erase aborted trials or interpolate invalid gaps to complete a favourable series. Preserve missingness and every exclusion's provenance.
5. **Freeze quantitative acceptance and uncertainty.** Name primary error metrics, dimensional or normalized error denominator, domain-specific numerical tolerances and their physical justification. Include measurement/calibration, uncertain inflow and parameter uncertainty plus numerical discretization uncertainty; do not add correlated errors as though independent. Compare predicted and observed quantities with a declared uncertainty model and report bias, residual structure, uncertainty interval coverage and condition-specific failures. RM1's reported combined measurement uncertainty is not automatically a solver pass tolerance. Do not choose a threshold from observed residuals. No arbitrary universal percent is supplied here.
6. **Freeze repeatability and sensitivity.** Separate independently prepared trials from serially correlated samples; account for autocorrelation and grouping in effective replication and intervals. Declare an achievable precision/power analysis using independent pilot evidence, or state the test is exploratory. Report between-run variation, calibration repeatability and sensitivity to valid preprocessing/numerical choices. Many time points or phase bins do not manufacture independent replicates.
7. **Commit predictions before unblinding.** Save immutable input/split/model manifests and timestamped, hashed predicted outputs before exposing test targets. Independently review the frozen plan and evidence boundaries first. Preserve all failures, exclusions and inconclusive runs; publish target comparison without retuning. A reproducible negative result is useful and must not be replaced by the best fitted run.
8. **Limit the conclusion.** At most establish predictive agreement for the tested observable and conditions with stated uncertainty. A separate campaign/scale/geometry needs separate validation. Neither an RM1 table identity nor an RM2 curve fit establishes an original theory, a device winner, Fundy site energy, annual production, fatigue life or full-scale certification.

## Source-specific release conditions before any current/power model

FORCE requires exact instrument serial/configuration, face elevation/tilt, cell centre versus edge reconciliation (1.5m label versus published 2m first centre), coordinate/declination lineage, source fill/zero policy, native per-beam diagnostics and scientifically justified time-varying surface/sidelobe/near-bed/retrieval masks. Validate mask logic against native evidence on a separate interval, then independently review the resulting fixed-depth coverage and uncertainty. A mask cannot be accepted just because it drops all zero rows. Do not alter historical quality counts or promote existing nonzero values.

UNH requires original instrument README/unit/licence evidence and calibration, offset/drift, clock epoch/timezone/synchronization, missing/QC encodings, common availability and test logs before selecting a small concurrent interval. Select upstream ADCP by direction and actual geometry; account for platform motion. Identify whether electrical channels are generator-, inverter- or grid-side, their signs and auxiliary inclusion; subtract only consumption not already included. Support load requires its own calibration and motion correction. Do not select an exact interval based only on campaign dates or substitute New Hampshire currents for a Nova Scotia berth. No new acquisition script or bulk download is authorized by this plan.

## Independent load-to-stress and deployment boundaries

A measured force or shaft torque history is not stress. Blade/strut stress requires calibrated strain or a validated transfer model with load paths, geometry, material and boundary conditions. Fatigue further requires applicable material curves, mean-stress/environment assumptions, cycle counting, uncertain exposure and independent validation. Net electricity requires actual synchronised metering after all relevant PTO/controller losses. Each needs its own holdout/uncertainty evidence; a power-only pass cannot unlock them.

No physical tests, hardware, spending, contacts, access credentials or field deployment are initiated. Ecology, fisheries/Mi'kmaq engagement, navigation/permits, manufacture, maintenance and retrieval remain separate decision gates. PR #8 and all UI/artwork are untouched.
