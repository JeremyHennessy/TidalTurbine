# FORCE 2018 native-instrument QC recovery — evidence gate

**Checked 2026-10-20. Decision: BLOCKED.** These notes refer to the upward-looking FAST3 / Signature 500, source instrument serial **100258**, in Minas Passage. The public full-campaign processed currents and companion waves have been SHA-256 verified and described in `docs/FORCE_FULL_CAMPAIGN_QA_20261020.md`; **not one current cell is accepted as physically valid turbine inflow**.

## Primary manufacturing documentation — useful, but not retroactive proof

Nortek's [Data files](https://support.nortekgroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/27060689928348-Data-files) (support article revised 2026-04-27, read 2026-10-20) distinguishes:

- `*.ad2cp`: single-ping **raw** data. Required for independent correlation/amplitude, heading/tilt and averaging review.
- `*_avgd.ad2cp`: averaged current data; internal instrument QC may already have been applied.
- `*.deploy`, `*.hdr`, `*.cfg`: instrument deployment settings and configuration/header, needed to resolve blanking, orientation, burst averaging and cell origin.

Nortek's [Internal data quality control](https://support.nortekgroup.com/hc/en-us/articles/27060647072156-Internal-data-quality-control) (revised 2026-04-27) documents for averaged Signature files that pings with **correlation below 50%** are discarded before averaging. The output includes *Percent Good* for retained pings per cell; cells with Percent Good below 50% are flagged with velocity **−32.767 m/s**, speed **46.34 m/s** and direction **225°**. These figures describe Nortek's documented processing conventions **as of the support article**, not independently verified settings or firmware behaviour of the 2018 instrument.

In the checksum-pinned FORCE processed NetCDF, none of the three ENU velocity arrays has any values equal to **−32.767 m/s**; all three components are exactly zero at **97,911 identical time/range positions**. The native data-quality fields `percent_good`, `beam_correlation`, `beam_amplitude`, `tilt` and `heading` are **not present** as NetCDF variables. These findings are reproducible with `python -m scripts.build_force_quality_grid --archive <source.zip>`. They indicate a material provenance gap; **they do not establish that Nortek itself filled failed cells with zero**, nor do they prove that every zero was produced by the same conversion step. No rule converting zero into valid slack flow is supported.

## Publisher-side conversion and coordinates

The [CIOOS FORCE currents metadata](https://cioosatlantic.ca/erddap/info/FORCE_Mar2018_ADCP_Currents/index.html) records processing by `force/adcp/convert_force_nc.py` from a `cioos-atlantic/atlantic-data-transform` repository on **2022-01-13**. [CIOOS's public ERDDAP dataset configuration](https://github.com/cioos-atlantic/erddap/blob/master/datasets.d/FORCE_Mar2018_ADCP_Currents.xml) corroborates this history. The conversion repository/script was not available in the inspected public GitHub location, so its zero-fill substitutions, rotation conventions, unit conversion, ordering and quality masks **cannot be reconstructed authoritatively**. This is a **missing source-processing transformation**, not an assertion that the conversion is wrong.

The reviewed [Viehman et al. (2022)](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmars.2022.851400) deployment description reports the upward transducer ~**0.7 m above the seabed**, ~**1 m blanking** and a first measurement centre **2 m above the transducer**. The public product labels `cell_range = 1.5, 2.5, …, 50.5 m`. Without the original `.deploy`/`.cfg`, producer definitions and instrument reference point, this **0.5 m disagreement remains unresolved**. Do not add 0.7 m and a product range to assign certified absolute seabed elevation, or use dbar directly as chart-datum tide height.

## Exact source recovery sequence, in order

1. **Native archive and retrieval logs.** Inspect or recover the original `.ad2cp` single-ping file, `*_avgd.ad2cp` if generated, `.deploy`, `.cfg`, `.hdr`, source fieldnotes and instrument recovery/deployment timestamps for serial 100258. CIOOS advertises [native archive](https://data.fundyforce.ca/forceCloud/index.php/s/TAEz9gwARDDXCTD?path=%2F) and [metadata archive](https://data.fundyforce.ca/forceCloud/index.php/s/85etc2wCZt7K9FA), but bounded public HEAD probes both returned HTTP 500 in [GitHub Actions 39495696237](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/TidalTurbine/actions/runs/39495696237). Web retrieval was also unavailable during this pass. This does **not** demonstrate permanent unavailability; do not claim a download succeeded. A second **bounded GET** probe (not HEAD), [GitHub Actions run 39519202400](https://github.com/JeremyHennessy/TidalTurbine/actions/runs/39519202400), also returned **HTTP 500 on both endpoints** at 2026-10-21 00:05 UTC, without downloading raw bytes. This confirms the present access blocker from two HTTP methods, not permanent unavailability.
2. **Source-code transformation.** Recover the exact 2022 version, commit checksum and dependencies of `convert_force_nc.py`, plus any intermediate converted CSV/MAT and QC log. Determine what the zero vector means (masked above-surface/near-bottom, unmeasured, conversion fill or other).
3. **Native beam-level checks.** Confirm 2018 instrument firmware, Percent Good/correlation/echo/beam amplitude, pitch/roll/heading, raw burst duration and sample count, transducer/sidelobe masks, surface pressure handling, and range-centre geometry. Preserve raw flags before aggregation; don't set missing vectors to zeros.
4. **Cross-check both products.** Recompute representative processed burst vectors from original bytes across low/high tide, valid/suspect cell boundaries and May 23–24 recovery transition; compare to NetCDF with uncertainty and documented unit/rotation conventions. Native QC must also flag the terminal low-pressure intervals, not blindly use an analyst's <20 dbar screen.
5. **Quality/engineering acceptance.** Publish a versioned cell/time mask with `valid`/`missing`/`suspect`/`rejected`, native evidence hashes and physical depth uncertainty. Only specifically approved cell/time regions could be considered for matched profile-averaging after independent engineering review. A 56-day spring campaign cannot establish annual extremes or full-scale turbine lifetime loads.

**Owner-independent stop condition:** If native records or transformation remain unavailable, the correct result is `SOURCE_QC_BLOCKED`, **zero model-eligible FORCE observations**, not an interpolated or cosmetically cleaned velocity field. Current numeric heatmaps are visual QC diagnostics only.
